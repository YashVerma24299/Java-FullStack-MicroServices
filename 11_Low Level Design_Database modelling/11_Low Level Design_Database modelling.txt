2.Templatisation Service
    - A Templatisation Service (or Template Service) is a service responsible for -> Generating dynamic content using predefined templates.
    - It replaces placeholders/variables inside templates with actual runtime data.
    - Example Template:
        - Hello {{name}}, Your OTP is {{otp}}.
        - At runtime:
            - name = Yash
            - otp = 4582
        - After replacement:
            - Hello Yash,Your OTP is 4582.
        - This replacement process is called: Templatisation
